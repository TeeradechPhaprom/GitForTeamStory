## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a **castle** so old that its front door had a "Please Knock Twice" sign and a doorbell that had given up years ago.  
   ![castle](img/castle.png)

2. One morning, a **dragon** swooped down from the clouds, breathing fire and roaring. Everyone screamed. Then it sneezed, and a small puff of steam came out, along with a loud *beep-boop*. "It's not a dragon," whispered the cook. "It's a **robot**!" Both were right. It was a robot dragon named Sparky, built by the castle's scientist, Dr. Pimm.
 ![dragon](img/dragon.png)

3. Dr. Pimm ran out waving her arms and shouted, "**Whatever you do, don't press the big red button!**" The royal scribe was busy eating a sandwich and wrote nothing down. This turned out to be a mistake. ![scientist](img/scientist.png)

4. Naturally, the cat pressed the big red button. Everyone, including the dragon, the cook, and the scribe, was teleported (or maybe just very lost) into a dusty cave. In the middle sat a **treasure chest**, and it was snoring.  
   ![treasure chest](img/treasure_large.png) 

5. The chest woke up. Out climbed a very sleepy pirate with a parrot hat. Taped inside the lid was a **map**. It had a big red X, a drawing of a tree, and the words "Good luck!" There were no directions anywhere. "That's the whole map," the pirate said proudly. "I like to keep things mysterious." ![treasur open](img/treasure_open.png)![ghost](img/ghost.png) ![map](img/map.png)

6. "I'm Captain Pickles," said the pirate, "and I've been napping in this chest for three hundred years. I'm tired of treasure. **Let's go to space!**" Nobody could think of a good reason to say no. ![pirate](img/pirate.png)

7. Dr. Pimm built a rocket out of the castle's chimney in under five minutes. It **blasted off**, and at the same moment her **time machine** broke down with a sad *clunk*. The rocket flew up, hung in the air for a second, and then fell back into the forest, because the time machine had been holding the whole plan together. 
![rocket](img/rocket.png)

8. Everyone sat in the leaves, dizzy and covered in soot. Then the cat finally spoke. "Before anyone asks: yes, I pressed the button. No, I'm not sorry. You were all late with my dinner." ![cat](img/cat.png)

9. In the forest, the **detective**, who had been following the group for no clear reason, discovered... **a tree with a big red X carved into it**. Under it was a small, dusty box. Inside was Dr. Pimm's missing note from earlier, which said: "If the cat is near the red button, give the cat a snack."![detective](img/detective.png)

10. Everyone looked at the cat. The cat looked at everyone. The cook ran back to the castle and returned with a bowl of tuna. The cat ate it and purred. Suddenly a green alien strolled past, completely calm, holding three ice cream cones that were dripping everywhere. It paused, looked at everyone for one second, said, "Sorry, wrong planet," and kept walking without anyone daring to ask a single question. The top scoop fell right onto Captain Pickles's head. He licked it at once and announced, "Strawberry! Best thing I've tasted in three hundred years!" Then the cat pushed the button on Dr. Pimm's repaired time machine and sent everyone home. Back at the castle, Sparky guarded the door, Captain Pickles moved into the guest room, and the scribe finally wrote it all down, starting with a note on the fridge: "Feed the cat on time."![alien](img/alien.png)

The End.
