## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a **castle** so old that its front door had a "Please Knock Twice" sign and a doorbell that had given up years ago.  
   ![castle](img/castle.png)

2. One morning, a **dragon** swooped down from the clouds, breathing fire and roaring. Everyone screamed. Then it sneezed, and a small puff of steam came out, along with a loud *beep-boop*. "It's not a dragon," whispered the cook. "It's a **robot**!" Both were right. It was a robot dragon named Sparky, built by the castle's scientist, Dr. Pimm.

3. Dr. Pimm ran out waving her arms and shouted, "**Whatever you do, don't press the big red button!**" The royal scribe was busy eating a sandwich and wrote nothing down. This turned out to be a mistake.

4. Naturally, the cat pressed the big red button. Everyone, including the dragon, the cook, and the scribe, was teleported (or maybe just very lost) into a dusty cave. In the middle sat a **treasure chest**, and it was snoring.  
   ![treasure chest](img2.png)

5. The chest woke up. Out climbed a very sleepy pirate with a parrot hat. Taped inside the lid was a **map**. It had a big red X, a drawing of a tree, and the words "Good luck!" There were no directions anywhere. "That's the whole map," the pirate said proudly. "I like to keep things mysterious."

6. "I'm Captain Pickles," said the pirate, "and I've been napping in this chest for three hundred years. I'm tired of treasure. **Let's go to space!**" Nobody could think of a good reason to say no. 

7. A rocket blasted off, but at the same time the **time machine** broke down.
